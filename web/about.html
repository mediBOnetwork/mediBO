mediBO

B2B pharmacy ordering platform

mediBO is a business-to-business ordering platform built for licensed pharmacies and hospitals in India. mediBO lets verified pharmacy and hospital buyers order medicines and surgical supplies directly from approved wholesale suppliers and distributors, with live stock availability and order tracking.

What mediBO does

  • Lets licensed pharmacies and hospitals browse and order medicines and surgical products.
  • Connects buyers to verified wholesale suppliers with real-time stock availability.
  • Tracks orders from placement through fulfilment and delivery.
  • Provides secure sign-in for verified business accounts.

Who it is for

mediBO is intended only for licensed pharmacy businesses, hospitals, and registered wholesale drug suppliers operating in India.
